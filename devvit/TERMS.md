_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

These terms cover the setlistbot Reddit app ("setlistbot", "the app"). By installing the app in a community, or by mentioning it or posting a show date where it's installed, you agree to them.

## What the app does

setlistbot reads new comments and posts in the Reddit communities where moderators have installed it. When it finds a show date, it replies with that show's setlist.

## Setlist data

Setlists come from third parties: [phish.net](https://phish.net) for Phish, [kglw.net](https://kglw.net) for King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, and a Grateful Dead setlist dataset that ships with the app. setlistbot doesn't own this data and can't guarantee that it's complete or accurate. Each reply links to its source.
