# setlistbot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

This policy explains what the setlistbot Reddit app ("setlistbot", "the app") does with data.

## What the app reads

When a comment or post is created in a community where setlistbot is installed, Reddit sends the app that comment or post, including its text, its ID, and the author's username. The app uses them to:

- find show dates in the text
- check whether the text mentions setlistbot, if the community requires a mention
- avoid replying to its own comments

The text and username are not stored, logged, or shared.

## What the app stores

The ID of each comment or post it replies to, so it never replies twice. IDs are kept in Reddit-hosted storage that belongs to that community's installation of the app.

## What the app sends elsewhere

To look up a setlist, the app sends the show date, and nothing else, to:

- [phish.net](https://phish.net) (`api.phish.net`), for Phish
- [kglw.net](https://kglw.net), for King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard

No usernames, comment text, or other personal information leave Reddit. Grateful Dead setlists are included in the app, so those lookups send nothing anywhere.

## What the app doesn't do

No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and no selling or sharing of data.

## Changes

This policy may change. The latest version is always at this page, along with its revision history.
